# Website Artwork

Gentoo artwork and the pixel forest background were supplied by the team. The
wordmark and small G mark have true PNG transparency; white artwork highlights
are retained. These files do not depend on CSS blending or AI generation.

Sponsor logos were retrieved from official sources on November 10, 2026 and are
served locally. Logos remain the property of their respective organizations.
