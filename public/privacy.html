Gentoo Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 28, 2026

Gentoo is a team operations app for Gentoo Robotics. It helps approved team members coordinate scouting, tasks, pit schedules, team notes, notifications, and related competition workflows.

Information We Collect

  • Account information such as name, email address, profile photo, role, and approval status.
  • Team-created content such as scouting notes, match reports, tasks, schedules, talkie requests, and notifications.
  • Device push notification tokens when a user enables push notifications.
  • Images selected for the cable counter feature, which are sent to an AI service for analysis when the user chooses to analyze a photo.

How We Use Information

  • To sign users in and restrict the app to approved team members.
  • To show and sync team scouting, scheduling, task, and communication data.
  • To deliver team notifications and push alerts.
  • To analyze cable photos when the user intentionally uses the cable counter feature.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Gentoo uses service providers to operate the app, including Supabase for authentication and database storage, Expo for app updates and push notification delivery, Google for Google sign-in, and Groq for the optional cable counter image analysis feature. Data is shared with these providers only as needed to provide the app's functionality.

Data Retention

Team data is retained while it is useful for team operations or until an administrator removes it. Users may request account or data removal by contacting the team administrator.

Children's Privacy

Gentoo is intended for members of a robotics team and is used under team supervision. The app is not intended for general public use.
